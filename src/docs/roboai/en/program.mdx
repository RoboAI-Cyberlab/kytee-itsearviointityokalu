<PracticeTitle name="program-1a">The organization has a cybersecurity strategy.</PracticeTitle>

### Significance

A cybersecurity strategy is a key document that defines the organization's cybersecurity direction and helps:
- **Create a consistent and goal-oriented approach to cybersecurity.** The strategy ensures that cybersecurity is planned, not reactive.
- **Protect the organization's assets and data repositories from cyber threats.** Preventing attacks reduces business risks and protects reputation.
- **Support compliance with legislation and regulatory requirements.** For example, **GDPR, NIS2, and ISO 27001** require systematic cybersecurity management.
- **Define clear responsibilities and resources for implementing cybersecurity.** Without defined roles and responsibilities, information security can become fragmented.
- **Enable continuous improvement of cybersecurity and maturity level development.** The strategy helps the organization evolve and adapt to changing threats.

Assessment questions:
- Does the organization have a written cybersecurity strategy or action plan?
- Does the strategy include cybersecurity objectives and related actions?
- Does the strategy define responsibilities and roles for implementing cybersecurity measures?
- Does the strategy follow recognized frameworks such as C2M2, NIST CSF, ISO 27001, or IEC 62443?
- Is the strategy updated and aligned with the organization's overall business strategy?

Implementing cybersecurity strategy in the organization:

1. **Strategy definition and objectives**
   - The cybersecurity strategy includes **clear objectives and action plans**.
   - Objectives may relate to **cyber risk management, identity and access management, event monitoring, and continuity assurance**.

2. **Action plan and framework utilization**
   - The strategy is based on **recognized frameworks** such as **C2M2, NIST CSF, ISO 27001, IEC 62443**.
   - **A C2M2-based strategy may include different domains**, such as risk identification, cyber incident response, and partner network security management.

3. **Responsibilities and resources**
   - **Roles and responsibilities are defined in the strategy**, such as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), IT security specialists, and business units.
   - **Resources and budget** are allocated to cybersecurity so measures can be implemented effectively.

4. **Monitoring and continuous improvement**
   - Strategy implementation is evaluated **regularly through audits, self-assessments, and maturity level analyses**.
   - The strategy is **updated regularly** to respond to changing cyber threats and business needs.

<AnswerOptions>
- **Not implemented or no information**: The organization does not have a formal cybersecurity strategy or its status is unknown.
- **Partially implemented**: A strategy has been developed, but it is not comprehensive or has not been systematically implemented.
- **Mostly implemented**: A strategy exists and is being implemented, but needs development, such as updates or better integration with business strategy.
- **Fully implemented**: The organization has a comprehensive and up-to-date cybersecurity strategy that includes objectives, actions, responsibilities, and monitoring.
</AnswerOptions>

### Examples from different industries

> **Food company**
The food company's cybersecurity strategy focuses on supply chain security and protecting automation systems. It is based on the ISO 27001 standard and C2M2 model.

> **Water utility**
The water utility utilizes IEC 62443 standards and NIST CSF framework as the basis for its strategy. Key focus areas are SCADA system protection and incident management.

> **Power company**
The energy company's cybersecurity strategy focuses on power grid security and ensuring cybersecurity continuity. Risk analyses and threat models are regularly integrated into the strategy.

### Additional information

- **The strategy should include concrete measures and objectives that support the organization's business strategy.**
- **A C2M2 model-based strategy helps structure cybersecurity domains and development objectives.**
- **Strategy implementation should be measured and evaluated regularly, for example through maturity level assessments.**
- **The cybersecurity strategy should be aligned with the organization's other strategic objectives.**
- **The strategy must not be a static document but should continuously evolve according to changing threats and requirements.**

---

<PracticeTitle name="program-2a">The organization's senior management supports cybersecurity management.</PracticeTitle>

### Significance

Senior management support is essential for cybersecurity management success because:
- **Without management support, cybersecurity easily becomes an isolated IT function and does not integrate with business strategy.**
- **Cybersecurity requires resources that management can provide.** If insufficient investment is made in cybersecurity, the organization is exposed to greater risks.
- **Management serves as an example for the entire organization.** When management commits to cybersecurity, other personnel also see its significance.
- **Legislation and regulation require management accountability for cybersecurity.** For example, **NIS2, GDPR, and ISO 27001** emphasize management responsibility for information security management.
- **Management support enables effective decision-making in cybersecurity.** Without clear decision-making and authority, cybersecurity measures can remain ineffective.

Assessment questions:
- Does senior management have a clear understanding of cybersecurity significance and risks?
- Does senior management provide adequate resources (personnel, tools, and funding) for cybersecurity management?
- Does senior management have the authority and responsibility to advance cybersecurity measures?
- Does senior management participate in defining, approving, and monitoring the cybersecurity strategy?
- Are cybersecurity objectives tracked and measured at the management level?

Implementing management support in the organization:

1. **Management participation in cybersecurity**
   - Senior management participates in **defining and approving the cybersecurity strategy**.
   - Management roles and responsibilities in cybersecurity are clearly defined.

2. **Providing resources for cybersecurity**
   - **Budget and personnel resources are adequate for cybersecurity management.**
   - **Necessary technologies and training** are allocated for cybersecurity implementation.

3. **Authority and responsibilities**
   - Senior management has **authority to make decisions on cybersecurity investments and measures**.
   - The organization has defined **clear responsibilities and reporting lines** related to cybersecurity management.

4. **Monitoring and measurement**
   - Management monitors **cybersecurity situational awareness and reporting regularly**.
   - **Cybersecurity assessments and risk evaluations** support management decision-making.

5. **Continuous improvement**
   - Management participates in **regular information security training and exercises**.
   - Cybersecurity objectives are evaluated **as part of the organization's strategic planning**.

<AnswerOptions>
- **Not implemented or no information**: There is no senior management support for cybersecurity, or its status is unknown.
- **Partially implemented**: Management is partially involved in cybersecurity, but does not provide sufficient resources or support for measures.
- **Mostly implemented**: Management supports cybersecurity and provides resources, but there is room for improvement in participation, monitoring, or decision-making.
- **Fully implemented**: Management actively supports cybersecurity, participates in strategic decision-making, and ensures the organization has adequate resources and processes for cybersecurity management.
</AnswerOptions>

### Examples from different industries

> **Food company**
The food company's management is committed to developing supply chain information security and production equipment cybersecurity. The budget has a separate allocation for information security training and technical solutions.

> **Water utility**
The water utility's management has set system information security as one of the organization's strategic priorities. Management monitors the cybersecurity situation and organizes exercises.

> **Power company**
At the energy company, management regularly participates in cybersecurity steering group meetings and ensures adequate resource allocation for protecting IT and OT environments.

### Additional information

- **Management support is critically important for cybersecurity success.**
- **Resources such as budget, personnel, and training are essential for cybersecurity implementation.**
- **Management should monitor cybersecurity situational awareness and risks regularly.**
- **Information security training and exercises are an important part of developing management cybersecurity awareness.**
- **Integrating cybersecurity into business strategy ensures that information security is not an isolated part of organizational operations.**
